                      VII. C ONCLUSION
   In this project, we successfully designed, simulated, and
verified a 4×4 SRAM array in 45 nm CMOS technology. The
work was distributed among team members to handle specific
blocks, resulting in a modular and integrated final design.
   A key achievement of this work is the highly optimized
layout. The single SRAM bitcell occupies an area of only
2.99 µm2 , designed with a pull-up ratio of 0.5 and a cell
(pull-down) ratio of 1.5 to ensure stability and robust noise
margins. The total top-module layout, inclusive of decoders,
