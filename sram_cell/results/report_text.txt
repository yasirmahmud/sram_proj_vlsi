                              45 nm Six-Transistor SRAM Cell
 Design and local EDA validation • November 7, 2026 • Single-cell deliverable

 A cross-coupled 6T SRAM cell was implemented in the Cadence GPDK045 process at 1.1 V. The final sized cell and
 the all-default-width assignment baseline have native Virtuoso schematics, symbols, and layouts. Both layouts
 pass the installed default GPDK045 DRC deck and match their respective schematics in LVS.

 The scope is one SRAM bitcell. The full 2×6 or 4×6 array, decoders, sense amplifier, and array CLK1-to-output
 measurements in Final Project Slides.pdf are subsequent integration work and are not claimed here.

 Sizing and interface
 The assignment baseline uses 120 nm for all six widths. The final project permits sizing to avoid write failure and
 destructive read. The supplied reference uses 120/240/360 nm; this was retained after characterization. The
 pull-down/access ratio is 1.5 and the access/pull-up ratio is 2. NMOS bulks connect to GND; PMOS bulks connect to
 Vdd. Q follows BL when WL is high. Q and Q_bar are exposed as observation ports; ordinary operation only drives
 WL, BL, BL_bar, and the supplies.

 The two cells are sram_cell/sram_cell and sram_cell/sram_cell_default in the local library. The reusable symbols
 expose BL, BL_bar, WL, Q, Q_bar, Vdd, and GND.

 Standalone layout and extraction




 Compact native Virtuoso layout matching the requested reference arrangement. Contacts are 0.06 × 0.06 µm.

 The layout has one connected N-well with a Vdd tap and a substrate tap tied to GND. All 42 streamed poly
 rectangles are vertically oriented. The two inverters occupy the center, with staggered access transistors at the
 left and right sides, matching the requested reference arrangement. Metal1 provides local device connections.
 Three horizontal Metal2 straps replace horizontal poly interconnects so that all poly remains vertical. The
 boundary is 2.120 × 1.680 µm (3.5616 µm²), including standalone taps and contacted wordline access. This
 replaces the previous 15.990 µm² floorplan.

 Physical read, retention, and corner validation
 A separate 50 MHz experiment writes 10110010 through external transmission gates, disables the write drivers,
 and retains the bit. Both bitlines are then precharged to VDD with the wordline low. Precharge turns off before the
 wordline rises for read. Q and Q_bar are never externally driven in this test. Each bitline has 20 fF capacitance.




 Eight 20 ns cycles. Write WL is high at 2.1–5 ns; precharge at 9.1–11 ns; read WL at 12.1–15 ns, relative to each
 cycle. All transitions take 100 ps.

 Assertions check write completion at 4.8 ns, hold at 8 ns, read at 14.8 ns, and retention after read at 19 ns. Valid
 rails are below 10% or above 90% of VDD. Both bitlines must reach at least 99% of VDD before read. Read
 differential must have the correct sign and exceed 100 mV; the disturbed low storage node must remain below
 VDD/2.

 Supply scaling and deterministic gate offset
 The 800 ns write stimulus is repeated at five coarse supplies from 0 to 1.1 V, then refined around failure. VDD also
 scales the WL/BL source amplitudes. The offset source is in one feedback path, with V(Q_gate) − V(Q) = voffset.
 This is the assignment gate-offset experiment, not a statistical threshold mismatch model.

 At every supply, both Q and Q_bar are checked against their expected values in the write and hold intervals. The
 score is min(Vhigh/VDD, 1 − Vlow/VDD) over all sampled states. PASS requires a score of at least 0.90. Zero
 supply is explicitly a failure. The no-offset boundary is bracketed between 0.33 V (fails) and 0.34 V (passes), for
 this waveform and criterion.

 Read and write static margins
 DC VIN is swept from 0 to 1.1 V in 0.5 mV steps. Read SNM is the smaller largest axis-aligned square in the two
 mirrored VTC lobes, with WL = BL = VDD. The square is computed from the decreasing curve boundaries and
 plotted explicitly. For write, the two VTCs use BL = VDD and BL = 0, following the handout’s square construction.
 This write metric is identified separately from a write-trip voltage or statistical write margin.

 checks finish with zero results for both cell variants. Optional DFM, density, and antenna decks are separate from
 this default run. Both LVS reports state MATCH, including exact drawn W and L checks. Both native schematic
 checks return (0 0).
